About me

I am a student at Yerevan State University, majoring in Informatics (Computer Science). I work on machine learning, algorithms, and competitive programming. I have participated in the IOI, IMO, IMC, and the ICPC World Finals, and I am currently exploring neural-network-guided approaches to automated theorem proving. I recently created an Armenian typeface and resumed playing music. This site is a brief introduction to me and my work.
